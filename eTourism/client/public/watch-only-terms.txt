WATCH-ONLY TICKET — TERMS

1. WHAT THIS TICKET INCLUDES
The Watch-only ticket grants you access to the live video and audio stream of the experience for its full scheduled duration. You will be able to see and hear the e-host and watch any content the e-host shares on screen.

2. WHAT THIS TICKET DOES NOT INCLUDE
Watch-only ticket holders cannot send messages, ask questions, speak in the live session, or otherwise interact with the e-host or other viewers. The Q&A and chat features are reserved for Normal ticket holders. If you would like to interact with the e-host during the experience, please book a Normal ticket instead.

3. JOINING THE LIVE SESSION
You will receive a viewer link by email after booking. The link will be activated shortly before the scheduled start time. We recommend joining a few minutes early to confirm your connection.

4. REPLAY ACCESS
A replay of the live session will be made available to you for 24 hours after the experience ends, subject to the e-host's permission and any technical limitations.

5. REFUND POLICY
The same cancellation and refund rules apply as for Normal tickets:
- 100% refund for cancellations made 14 or more days before the event.
- 50% refund for cancellations made 7 to 13 days before the event.
- No refund for cancellations made fewer than 7 days before the event.
We do not refund Watch-only tickets on the basis that you could not interact with the e-host — this limitation is the defining feature of this tier.

6. AGE REQUIREMENT
All viewers using a Watch-only ticket must be 18 years of age or older. The buyer is responsible for ensuring everyone who watches the stream meets this requirement.

7. CONDUCT
Although you cannot interact during the live session, you remain bound by the platform's general Terms of Service and conduct rules, including those covering recording, redistribution, and respectful use of the service.

8. RECORDING AND PRIVACY
Sessions may be recorded for quality, safety, and dispute resolution as described in the main Terms of Service. By joining the stream you consent to such recording.
